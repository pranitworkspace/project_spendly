Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Spendly keeps a record of your spending, so we take the handling of that information seriously. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what control you have over it.

1.Data We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run your account and show you your spending. That amounts to:

  • Account details — the name and email address you register with, and a securely hashed version of your password.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter yourself.
  • Basic usage information — such as the date your account was created and when you last signed in.

We do not ask for, and you should never enter, bank account numbers, card numbers, or other payment credentials. Spendly does not connect to your bank.

2.How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to:

  • Display your expenses, category breakdowns, and monthly summaries.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Filter and sort your records by the date ranges you choose.
  • Diagnose faults and keep the service running correctly.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it with advertisers. We do not use your expense records to build a marketing profile of you, and we will not send you marketing email you did not ask for.

3.Data Storage

Your records are stored in the application's database and are tied to your account, so that only a session signed in as you can read them. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and are never held in plain text.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to close your account, the associated expense records are removed along with it.

No system can promise perfect security. While we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, you should keep your own copies of records that matter to you.

4.Third Party Services

Spendly is deliberately light on external dependencies. The third parties involved in running it may include a hosting provider that operates the servers your data sits on, and a web font provider used to load the typefaces on these pages.

These providers process data only to the extent needed to deliver their part of the service, and they are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We do not run advertising networks, third-party analytics, or social media tracking pixels on this site.

We use cookies only for the essential purpose of keeping you signed in during a session. We do not use tracking cookies.

5.Contact Us

You may ask us at any time to show you the data held about you, correct something that is wrong, or delete your account and its records.

For any question about this policy or about your data, write to us at privacy@spendly.example and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

If we change this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
